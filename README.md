**About**

Marbles of the Universe is an interactive XR experience built with A-Frame. It takes place in a surreal cosmic environment where users are invited to explore a floating universe made up of glowing marbles.

Each marble acts as an interactive element. When clicked, it reveals a quote that contributes to a larger narrative a user can explore and interact. These fragments gradually unfold into a symbolic story about memory of the universe.

The experience is designed to feel immersive and exploratory. Objects are not static, they exist in a 3D space that users can navigate freely. By interacting with the environment, users slowly reveal how individual universe come together to from a, smaller objects.

**Interaction**

Click on glowing marbles to reveal hidden quotes and narrative fragments
Move through the 3D space using WASD controls.
Look around and explore the environment freely.
